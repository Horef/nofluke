# Survey of existing tools (2026-11)
Status: current (verified 2026-11-17)

Why nofluke exists (D1): what about 20 existing optimisation tools offer for expensive, noisy evaluations run outside Python, and what none of them does. Written when the old Genetic Optimization Engine (GOE) was reviewed for revival.

Three web-research agents, versions from PyPI/GitHub on 2026-11-17. "tested" = run by the agent in a scratch venv.

## Evidence on algorithms
- NeurIPS 2020 BBO challenge (Turner et al. 2021, arXiv 2104.10201), 128 evals: TuRBO 88.9, skopt 88.1, Ax 87.0, TPE 82.4, Nevergrad(1+1) 80.0, pycma 78.7, OpenTuner 76.9, random 75.8. Authors: EAs "clearly not competitive", "generally require thousands of evaluations".
- Raponi et al. 2023 (arXiv 2310.00077): BO wins at low budgets; CMA/NGOpt overtake as budget grows.
- Overtuning (Schneider, Bischl, Feurer, AutoML 2025, arXiv 2506.19540): optimising a noisy validation score can pick configs worse on test; ~10% of cases worse than default; worst on small data.
- Noisy EI (Letham et al. 2019, arXiv 1706.07094); racing: irace (López-Ibáñez 2016), SMAC3 (Lindauer et al. JMLR 2022).
- HPOBench (Eggensperger et al. 2021, arXiv 2109.06716): multi-fidelity helps at small compute, fades at large; caveat: low-fidelity rankings must correlate with final ones, risky when noise ≈ gap.

## Gaps no tool covers (all three agents agree)
1. File-driven batch CLI (init / ask --n / tell results.json / status / best) for evaluators outside Python.
2. Determinism: same seed + same tells -> same candidates across processes/restarts.
3. A noise protocol as a first-class operation: evaluation seed passed to the evaluator, re-evaluate top-k with fresh seeds on demand, aggregate mean / n / LCB, select by LCB not max.
4. Selection-vs-report discipline (held-out test never used for selection).
5. Serialisable space with conditionals in a file (only ConfigSpace has this).
6. Append-only machine-readable history with provenance (seed, parent, operator, job id, strategy version).
7. Failure policy (retry same seed once, stale RUNNING trials from killed jobs).
